## What's new

- **Far fewer rate-limit errors.** TokenTide now polls the usage endpoint every 10 min while Claude Code is active and every 30 min when idle. On a 429 it backs off for 10 → 20 → 40 → 60 min, and remembers the backoff across relaunches. Throttling stays silent while the numbers are under an hour old; the footer shows their age instead.
- **Fixed a retry loop.** After a window reset, the app could retry every minute while it was throttled.
- **Plans without a 5-hour limit (Ultra).** The ring now shows whichever limits your plan has; the 7-day limit moves to the outer ring.
- **Plan name stays current after an upgrade.** It's read from your profile every 6 h. Refresh forces a check.
- **New models show up automatically** in the model mix, and per-model weekly limits like "7d Fable" are shown too.
- The model mix is now labeled **Claude Code only**. Desktop app and claude.ai usage counts toward your limits but isn't logged locally.

**Tip:** for live numbers with no polling at all, right-click the widget and choose **Use Claude Code Status Line**.
